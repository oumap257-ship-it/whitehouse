Welcome to Kakamega

A Secluded Sanctuary Built for Rest, Warmth & Privacy.

Tucked along a peaceful, tree-lined access road off the Kisumu-Kakamega Highway in Amalemba, The White House Kakamega blends residential privacy with boutique hotel polish. Whether you are visiting Western Kenya for business, family gatherings, or an expedition into the Kakamega Rainforest, our gated retreat offers a tranquil home away from home.

Inside our secure perimeter walls, guests enjoy thoughtfully curated living lounges, natural timber-clad ceilings, custom upholstered seating, and modern self-catering dining facilities—all just minutes from Kakamega's vibrant town center.
